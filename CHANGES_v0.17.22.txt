Hazz Karaoke Hoster v0.17.22
================================

Single Deck side-list interface
-------------------------------
- Single Deck + Side List Mode now hides the complete Deck 2 player panel explicitly, including its title, LED, Play, Pause, Stop, Shuffle, time display, seek control and volume control.
- Automatic display scaling can no longer make the hidden Deck 2 player reappear.
- A dedicated side-list control panel replaces the player with Add Files, Load List, Save List, Select All, Move Up, Move Down, Send to Deck 1, Shuffle List, Remove Selected and Clear List.
- Move and Send actions preserve the order of multi-selected tracks.
- Clear List confirms the action and never deletes music files or library records.
- Returning to normal mode restores the complete Deck 2 player and its standard playlist footer.

Music pause and seek
--------------------
- Pause now retains the loaded track and its exact playback position.
- The Pause button changes to Resume while paused.
- Pressing either Resume or Play continues the same track instead of restarting or consuming it.
- Automatic crossfade does not advance a deliberately paused deck.
- Both music players now have a draggable seek slider.
- The target time is shown while dragging and is applied when the slider is released.
- Keyboard seeking with arrows, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End is supported.
- Seeking a paused track updates the saved resume position.

Smooth karaoke-to-music handover
--------------------------------
- Fade Stop lowers karaoke audio over 1.5 seconds.
- Background music then fades in separately over 1.5 seconds.
- The fixed return fade avoids an instant volume jump regardless of the normal crossfade-time setting.

Direct Karaoke Deck loading
----------------------------
- Karaoke search results can be dragged directly onto the Karaoke Deck to load them.
- Supported ZIP, CDG, audio and video files can be dragged from Windows Explorer onto the Karaoke Deck.
- A dropped track is loaded for checking and does not begin playing automatically.
- Music-only search records and unsupported files are rejected.
- A new drop cannot interrupt karaoke that is already playing; use Fade Stop first.

VJ music-video output
---------------------
- Music and video folders can be imported together from the Import menu.
- When Deck 1 or Deck 2 plays a video file, its picture is mirrored full-screen to the singer/audience display.
- The audience copy is always muted while the normal music deck supplies the show audio.
- Play, pause, resume, seek and stop stay synchronized with the music deck.
- Karaoke playback takes priority automatically; the music video returns when interval music resumes.

Build and compatibility
-----------------------
- Version advanced to 0.17.22.
- Visual Studio 2026 Release x64 single-file publishing remains enabled.
- Existing v0.17.21 libraries, queues, settings and singer history remain compatible.
