Hazz Karaoke Hoster v0.16.1

BPM STUDIO DAILY HISTORY
- Detects BPM Studio dated played-song list files as history, including when stored in the normal Lists area.
- Collapses multiple physical snapshots/copies for the same calendar day, choosing the most complete copy.
- Imports each day into music_history and also creates a loadable BPM Daily History — YYYY-MM-DD playlist.
- Preserves BPM play order and genuine repeated plays of the same track within a day.
- Daily-history lists sort newest-first at the top of the Music Playlists & History window.
- BPM Studio source files remain read-only.

This revision is based on v0.16.0 and retains its laptop scaling, singer history search/drag fix, playback and library features.
