Dummy's Guide

The shortest safe route from opening Hazz to playing your first singer.

Download PDFOpen full manual

Three rules

  1. Keep Hazz controls on Display 1 and the audience picture on Display 2.
  2. Load and check the next singer before pressing Play.
  3. Do not disconnect song drives while Hazz is open.

Set up in ten minutes

  1. Connect the projector and set Windows to Extend these displays.
  2. Open Hazz Karaoke Hoster.exe.
  3. Choose Import, Import Karaoke Folders.
  4. Add Music Folders if you want interval music.
  5. Press TV and check the audience picture on Display 2.
  6. Choose Show, New Show.

Add and play a singer

  1. Type the name above the Karaoke Singers List and press Add Singer.
  2. Switch Search to Karaoke and find the request.
  3. Drag the result onto the singer, or directly onto the Karaoke Deck for standalone loading.
  4. Press Load Next Singer.
  5. Check the singer and song.
  6. Press the green Play button.
  7. Use Fade Stop for a 1.5-second karaoke fade followed by a 1.5-second music fade in.

Keep the queue fair

  • Drag singers or use Move Up and Move Down.
  • Set As Next puts the selected singer first.
  • Right-click and Hold someone who has gone outside.
  • Skip Once passes them one time.
  • Open the name dropdown and type to find a previous singer.

Run interval music

  • Use Shift-click, Ctrl-click, Ctrl+A or Select All to choose several Music results, then drag or add them together.
  • In normal mode, drag music to either deck and use Auto Crossfade.
  • For one player, choose Show, Single Deck + Side List Mode. The right panel becomes a dedicated list with Add, Load, Save, Select, Move, Send, Shuffle, Remove and Clear controls.
  • Pause keeps the track and exact position; press Pause or Play to resume. Drag the timeline to seek.
  • Deck 1 automatically starts the next queued track in single-deck mode.
  • Play Music resumes the next scheduled track after karaoke.
  • Space quick-plays a highlighted Music result; Space again stops it.

Make Display 2 look good

  • Open Display, Overlay and Next Singer Settings.
  • Choose next singers, fonts, colours and scroller position.
  • Choose one background or a whole slideshow folder.
  • Use Fit for the whole picture, Fill for edge-to-edge cropping, or Stretch.
  • Videos loop silently and GIF speed is adjustable.

If something goes wrong

  • No screen: confirm Windows sees Display 2, then press TV.
  • Missing song: reconnect its drive and rescan.
  • Broken red song: use another version and repair the file later.
  • Lyrics early or late: use CDG -0.25s or +0.25s.
  • Wrong pitch: use Key minus or plus.
  • Cramped controls: maximise Hazz or hide preview.

Finish safely

  1. Let current media stop.
  2. Close the main Hazz window.
  3. Answer Yes to the shutdown question.
  4. Wait for Hazz to close before removing drives.
