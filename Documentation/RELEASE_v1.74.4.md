# Hazz Karaoke Hoster v1.74.4 — Deck-to-deck drag

v1.74.4 adds direct drag-and-drop movement between the two music decks.

## Deck 1 ↔ Deck 2

- Drag any **queued** song from Deck 1 onto Deck 2.
- Drag any **queued** song from Deck 2 back onto Deck 1.
- The track is **moved**, not copied.
- Drop position controls where the track lands in the destination queue.
- The **NOW PLAYING** track is protected and cannot be dragged between decks.
- If you drop above a deck that is currently playing, the live track stays at #1 and the moved song is placed after it.
- Existing same-deck reordering and Single Deck + Side List dragging are retained.

All v1.74.3 music-length/Search Columns fixes, v1.74.2 BPM MP3-tag fixes and earlier v1.74.x Music Video features remain included.
