# Hazz Karaoke Hoster v1.74.6 — Rotation standing without a song

v1.74.6 refines the singer Rotation column introduced in v1.74.5.

## Rotation standing

- Every **non-HOLD singer** now receives a live standing such as **1 / 8, 2 / 8, 3 / 8**, even if they have not yet chosen a karaoke song.
- Singers without a song no longer show a dash in the Rotation column.
- **HOLD** singers remain visible but are not counted in the active rotation total.
- The **NEXT** badge still marks the first singer who actually has a queued song ready to perform, so standing and playback readiness remain distinct.
- Manual ordering and Automatic Fair Rotation continue to drive the displayed standing.

All v1.74.5 singer highlighting and earlier v1.74.x features remain included.
