# Rotation methods

Original manual rotation is the default. Open Show > Rotation Settings and explicitly enable software-managed rotation to use any automatic method. Selecting a method alone does not enable it. An explicitly saved choice is remembered.

- Round robin: one turn per ready singer per round, in arrival order.
- Closed rounds: newcomers become eligible next round.
- Newcomers at the end: append newcomers to the current round.
- Interleaved newcomers: one newcomer after the configured number of returning singers, when available; one turn per round.
- Longest waiting: oldest arrival or previous turn first, without round boundaries.
- Fewest turns: lowest started-turn count this show first.
- Group rotation: one song per named group per round; longest-waiting ready group member represents it. Ungrouped singers have individual slots. Select a singer in the main queue before opening settings to assign their group.
- Arrival order: original arrival order without rounds; offered separately from round robin.

Round robin and group rotation offer end-of-current-round, next-round or interleaved newcomer placement. Interleave spacing is adjustable from 1 to 10. Non-round methods use the chosen tie-breaker. Consecutive-turn protection is optional. Held singers and singers without songs stay behind ready singers. Current performers remain in their existing slot.

Rounds advance when no ready eligible singer remains. Holds and missing song requests do not indefinitely block round advancement. Turn counts, group assignments and round state are included in show recovery. New Show resets the round and turn records. Mode and placement choices are saved in the UI settings and venue profiles. Turns are counted at Play, including subsequently interrupted performances.
