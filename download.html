What changed?

  • Tempo follows the playing karaoke, music or music-video track. Save a track default or a singer/song preference. Unsaved changes reset after playback.
  • Enter adds a singer. TEMPO replaces the optional song-title box.
  • Clear an entire deck playlist while the current song continues.
  • Load Next Singer preserves the venue display until Play.
  • Full singer names, larger singer and heading fonts, separate text outlines and an adjustable scroller inset.
  • Windows or VLC audience video, crisp/smooth CD+G and adjustable main-console text remain included.

Build, audio processing, saved-preference, main-window and audience checks passed. Windows and VLC were checked for tempo, pause and seek. This is not a multi-hour live-show soak test.
