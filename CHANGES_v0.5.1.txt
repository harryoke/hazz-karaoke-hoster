HAZZ KARAOKE HOSTER v0.5.1
===========================

- Audience Next Singer overlay hidden during active/paused karaoke.
- Removed audience "Waiting for singer" placeholder when queue is empty.
- CD+G audience surface layered explicitly above inactive video surface.
- Added KARAOKE / MUSIC search modes; video karaoke is part of KARAOKE.
- Added large-folder IMPORT KARAOKE and IMPORT MUSIC with background scanning,
  batched SQLite transactions, progress, cancellation and rescan/upsert behavior.
- Main rotation changed to one singer row with an ordered Songs collection.
- Drag KARAOKE search results onto singer rows.
- Search results moved to left overlay so singer rows remain available as drop targets.
- Double-click singer opens Singer Songs & History window.
- Reorder singer songs by drag/drop or Up/Down buttons.
- Per-queued-song key and CD+G sync controls.
- Saved singer names repopulate the editable singer selector.
- Completed assigned karaoke performances are written to persistent singer history.
- Added LOAD NEXT SINGER.
- Completed singers rotate to the bottom if more songs remain; otherwise they leave
  the current show rotation but stay in the permanent singer database/history.
- Existing v0.4 auto-crossfade and karaoke/music-resume behavior retained.
