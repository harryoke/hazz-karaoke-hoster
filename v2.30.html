What's new in Hazz Karaoke Hoster v2.30

v2.30 includes the Live Stems engine in one Windows x64 download. AI Automix and beat analysis are not included.

Load tracks from local folders

Open FOLDERS, expand a drive and select a folder. Filter its filenames, then drag a file onto the karaoke or music deck. Use Ctrl or Shift to select several files for a music queue. No library import is required.

Mix two playing decks

In two-deck mode, press Play on both decks. The second starts muted. Drag the crossfader left for Deck 1, right for Deck 2, or between them to blend. Both tracks continue. Tick AUTO CROSSFADE to restore automation.

Mute or solo live stems

Open STEMS while a music track plays. Mute Vocals, Drums, Bass or Other, or select several Solo buttons to hear them together. Restore original clears the effect. Separation runs locally on the CPU, and can take several seconds to catch up. The original mix plays if a stem block isn't ready.

Playback improvements

Karaoke video preparation runs in the background. The audio paths request more buffering and karaoke waits for sustained drift before correcting its position.
