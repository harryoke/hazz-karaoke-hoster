Hazz Karaoke Hoster v0.17.21
================================

MUSIC SEARCH MULTI-SELECTION

- Shift-click selects a continuous range of Music search results.
- Ctrl-click adds or removes individual tracks from the selection.
- Ctrl+A and the new SELECT ALL button select every visible result.
- ADD TO DECK 1 and ADD TO DECK 2 queue the complete selection in displayed order.
- Dragging a selected Music result carries the complete selection to either deck.
- Batch queueing skips missing files, tags them as broken and reports the skipped count.
- Karaoke search remains single-selection so songs still drag safely onto one singer at a time.

KARAOKE FADE STOP

- The Karaoke Stop button is now labelled FADE STOP.
- Live karaoke audio fades smoothly for 1.5 seconds before playback ends.
- The fade applies to normal Windows playback and the live pitch-shift audio engine.
- Paused playback, media errors, shutdown and safety cleanup still stop immediately.
- Background music resumes only after the karaoke fade has completed.

SINGLE DECK + SIDE LIST MODE

- A new Show menu option keeps Deck 1 as the only music player.
- Deck 2 changes into a tall music side list with its playback controls hidden.
- Music searches, files and saved playlists can be staged in the side list.
- One or several selected side-list tracks can be dragged into Deck 1 in order.
- Crossfade controls are disabled in this mode and Deck 2 cannot start accidentally.
- Deck 1 automatically advances through its own playlist.
- The selected mode and side-list contents survive application restarts.

BUILD

- Version advanced to 0.17.21.
- Visual Studio 2026 Release x64 build validated.
