Hazz Karaoke Hoster v0.17.2 — Music Search SPACE Quick-Play + Safe Exit
===================================================================

MUSIC SEARCH SPACE-BAR QUICK PLAY
---------------------------------
- Highlight a MUSIC search result and press SPACE.
- If regular deck music is currently playing, Hazz fades it down while the highlighted track fades in.
- The highlighted search result is played directly; it is NOT added to Deck 1 or Deck 2.
- Press SPACE a second time to stop music completely.
- After the second SPACE Hazz remains idle and waits for either NEXT KARAOKE SONG or PLAY MUSIC.
- PLAY MUSIC exits quick-play cleanly and starts the next queued music track.
- Starting karaoke exits quick-play so quick music can never continue over a singer.
- Quick-play tracks are marked played-this-session and written to music play history as “Search Quick Play”.

SAFE EXIT / ACCIDENTAL SHUTDOWN PROTECTION
------------------------------------------
- Every normal attempt to close the main Hazz window now asks:
  “Close Hazz Karaoke Hoster? Any karaoke or music playback will stop.”
- Default response is No.
- Choosing No cancels shutdown and preserves the live show.
- Choosing Yes performs the existing clean-shutdown save and cleanup path.

BASELINE
--------
- Built directly from v0.17.1.
- The v0.17.1 one-click TV / Display 2 button remains unchanged.
