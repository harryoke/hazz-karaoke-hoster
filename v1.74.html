What’s New in Hazz Karaoke Hoster v1.74

A substantial update focused on music videos, Side List browsing and everyday usability.

Dedicated Music Video library

Import individual Music Video files or complete folders. Music Videos are now stored and browsed separately from ordinary Music, while existing indexed videos are automatically reclassified without moving the files.

Single Deck + Side List virtual folders

Browse Hazz virtual folders and imported BPM Studio folders directly from the Side List. Switch between Music and Music Videos, page through large folders, multi-select, drag tracks, add them to the Side List or send them straight to Deck 1.

Better Music Video presentation

Choose Fit or Stretch and select from Cut, Fade from Black/White, Flash White, four-direction Wipes, Zoom Reveal, Spin Zoom, Curtains, Diagonal Sweep, Neon Sweep and Random Fancy. Transition duration is adjustable and audio timing remains independent.

Search improvement

Close the search results and click back into the unchanged search box: your previous result set reopens immediately without needing another keystroke.

Playback fixes retained

Includes the LibVLC project fix and the Single Deck + Side List consecutive-video fix, along with karaoke A/V sync, audience preview, transparent CD+G backgrounds, Sound FX and earlier v1.7 improvements.
