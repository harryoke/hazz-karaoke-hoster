Hazz Karaoke Hoster v2.20

Made For KJ/DJ's By A KJ/DJ

v2.20 adds a ready-to-use Halloween audience bezel sample to every download. All v2.10 music effects, saved singer-list widths, Singer Database Manager, crossfade presets, Library Health Centre and earlier features remain included.

Halloween audience bezel

The included Templates/Hazz_Audience_Bezel_Halloween_1920x1080.png is an exact 1920 × 1080 transparent PNG. It has a black-and-purple Halloween border with pumpkins, bats, cobwebs and haunted houses, plus a large fully transparent centre for the audience display.

  1. Extract the complete Hazz download.
  2. Open DISPLAY.
  3. Tick Audience bezel / event frame.
  4. Choose Templates/Hazz_Audience_Bezel_Halloween_1920x1080.png.

Hazz detects the clear centre automatically and fits the full audience display inside it. Karaoke, CD+G, music video, singer information and both scrollers remain in the viewing area; the bezel always stays above them. No edge-position sliders are needed.

Make your own

The same Templates folder includes an editable 1920 × 1080 Photoshop template and simple guide. Keep the middle transparent, place artwork around the outer edge and export a transparent PNG.

Installation

Close older Hazz versions, extract v2.20 into a new folder and run Hazz Karaoke Hoster.exe. The header should show v2.20. Existing libraries, singers, venue profiles and audience settings remain in their normal locations.
