Hazz Karaoke Hoster v0.17.18
Audience screen fitting, scroller position and search drop targets

- Added singer-display background choices:
  - Fit: shows the complete image with letterboxing when needed.
  - Fill (crop): fills the entire audience screen while preserving aspect ratio.
  - Stretch: fills the entire audience screen without cropping.
  - Center: shows the media at its original size in the centre.
- The selected mode applies to still images, animated GIFs and muted background videos,
  including every item in a background slideshow.
- Added a Top/Bottom selector for the full singer-rotation scroller.
- The Next Singers panel automatically reserves space at the selected scroller edge.
- Music search results are shown over the centre workspace, keeping both music deck
  playlists visible and available as drag-and-drop targets. Karaoke search continues to
  leave the singer rotation visible as its drop target.
- All v0.17.17 long-show stability protections and earlier features remain intact.
